# Retail Sales Performance & Profitability Analysis
## Project Overview

This project uses Microsoft Power BI to analyze retail sales performance and profitability. The objective is to identify sales trends, profitable and underperforming products, customer and regional performance, and the relationship between discounting and profitability.

## Business Objectives
Analyze overall sales and profit performance
Identify top-performing products and sub-categories
Compare sales and profitability across regions
Analyze customer segment performance
Investigate the relationship between discounts and profit
Generate data-driven business insights and recommendations

## Tools & Technologies
Power BI
Power Query
DAX
Data Visualization
Data Modeling

## Dataset

The project uses the publicly available Superstore retail dataset.

Dataset source: Kaggle Superstore Dataset

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Sales Overview

Provides an overview of:

Total Sales
Total Profit
Total Orders
Profit Margin
Sales trends
Sales by category
Sales by region

### 2. Product & Profitability Analysis

Analyzes:

Top 10 products by sales
Top 10 products by profit
Profit by sub-category
Discount vs. profit
Product-level performance

### 3. Customer & Regional Analysis

Analyzes:

Sales by customer segment
Profit by customer segment
Sales by region
Profit by region

### 4. Business Insights & Recommendations

Summarizes key findings identified through the Power BI analysis and provides potential business recommendations.

## Data Preparation

Power Query was used to:

Validate data types
Check missing values
Investigate duplicate records
Validate date fields
Check sales and profit fields
Validate discount values
